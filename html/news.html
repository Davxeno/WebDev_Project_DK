New anime that just dropped!

New anime are releasing every week, and it's had to stay on top of it all, but as a profesionally unemployed chud let me tell you the hidden gems that released. I'm sure that no matter what sort of messed up stuff you're into, there's something here for you, whether that is slop, intense action or romance to bawl your eyes out. Lets see our debuting anime




Psyren: An adaptation of Toshiaki Iwashiro's manga, this series is a supernatural sci-fi thriller masterpiece. It follows a typical high school student, Ahega Yoshina, working a part time job to help those around town. One day in Persona 5 fashion, he receives a red calling card. At first he ignores it but it turned out his close friend Sakurako went missing after receiving an identical card. Calling the number on the card, he ends up transported into a lethat survival game in an alternate post-apocalypse version of Earth, where monsters called Taboo call home. To survive Ageha and Sakurako must master PSI and investigate the truth



Firefly Wedding: This dark historical romance and psychological drama is set during the Edo period. A wealthy and intelligent noblewoman, Satako Kirigaya suffers from a terminal heart condition. With her limited time left on this earth, her main wish is to marry a suitable man to make her father proud. One day she gets kidnapped by a group of mercenaries and brought before Shinpei Goto, a ruthless and psychopathic assassin assigned to execute her. Facing immitent death, she rolls the dice: she larps falling in loves with him and offers a fake marriage to buy herself time and stay alive. But, Shinpei actually accepts the proposal, fully infauated with her and vows to protect her as they flee into the wild on a dangerousd codependant journey, filled with dark romance and very fatal situations , where Satoko must fight and ensure her agency.




New Seasons Released

Your favourite shows have new seasons, so we can all rejoice as we get more absolute cinema from our favourite universes. After waiting for basically forever, we finally got new episodes, and 2026 continues to bring nothing but quality. There may even be some anime that you expected to be dead after one season but suprise, they got greenlit for another season while No Game No Life still sits at one season. Lets dive in!!




Cyberpunk Edgerunners Season 2: Netflix has returned to give us a new season of Cyberpunk Edgerunners promising us even more tears. In Umamusume fashion, we are treated to a whole new cast of characters meaning that there is another opportunity for us to see lives ruined by night city. You know me, I dont like excessive gore, excessive sexual content or tragic endings, and season 2 promises to bring all of that back... in a beautiful manner. It goes against everything I like and love it. it scratches the itch I didnt know I had. The Arasaka heist and Fall of King are excellent hooks to get you re-invested in night city, where happy endings dont exist



Apothecary diaries Season 3: Probably my most anticipated new season release of autumn, more political drama, a girl that loves being poisoned and illegal love is finally here. Apothecary diaries is absolute peak and if you havent seen it wtf are you doing? Maomao returns in style to investigate the mysteies of the imperial court, filled with imore of the same. I cant say enough how much I loved this season, but as always: bit of a sexual content warning. Its not as explicit as say Cyberpunk but it is very casual (with it being set in the court where the emperor has his concubines and all) which may make some people uncomfortable



Black Clover Season 2: Asta returns, adapting the high stakes Spade Kingdom arc. The dark triad are a mighty foe and the military rescue operation is a desparate attempt. Asta and the Magic Knights must stop the Qlipoth ritual the Triad is trying that is supposed to open the gateway to the underworld, which they are using captain Vangeance and captain Sukehiro to fuel it. What follows is an all out invasion as the remaining Magic knights regroup to rescue their captains. The season focuses on Asta's and Liebe's training and Yuno's heritage as well
