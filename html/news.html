New anime are releasing every week, and it's had to stay on top of it all, but as a profesionally unemployed chud let me tell you the hidden gems that released. I'm sure that no matter what sort of messed up stuff you're into, there's something here for you, whether that is slop, intense action or romance to bawl your eyes out. Lets see our debuting anime




Psyren: An adaptation of Toshiaki Iwashiro's manga, this series is a supernatural sci-fi thriller masterpiece. It follows a typical high school student, Ahega Yoshina, working a part time job to help those around town. One day in Persona 5 fashion, he receives a red calling card. At first he ignores it but it turned out his close friend Sakurako went missing after receiving an identical card. Calling the number on the card, he ends up transported into a lethat survival game in an alternate post-apocalypse version of Earth, where monsters called Taboo call home. To survive Ageha and Sakurako must master PSI and investigate the truth



Firefly Wedding: This dark historical romance and psychological drama is set during the Edo period. A wealthy and intelligent noblewoman, Satako Kirigaya suffers from a terminal heart condition. With her limited time left on this earth, her main wish is to marry a suitable man to make her father proud. One day she gets kidnapped by a group of mercenaries and brought before Shinpei Goto, a ruthless and psychopathic assassin assigned to execute her. Facing immitent death, she rolls the dice: she larps falling in loves with him and offers a fake marriage to buy herself time and stay alive. But, Shinpei actually accepts the proposal, fully infauated with her and vows to protect her as they flee into the wild on a dangerousd codependant journey, filled with dark romance and very fatal situations , where Satoko must fight and ensure her agency.
