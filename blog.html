The concept

At its most basic, Gachiakuta is an anime that asks a simple question. "How do people that been seen and discarded as trash by society act once they've been thrown away?" This in of itself is quite a deep and heavy topic, and the anime follows through. We see multiple different coping mechanisms in the anime, and how different people with different personalities would act in the situation. From Rudo's determination to climb back to from where he fell, to Zanka's apparent apathy or even Enjin's humour and high spirits in acceptance


Now lets talk about the plot a little. To explore such a heavy topic, the conceptual idea of the story itself must live up to it. And it does. It follows Rudo, a young boy who lives on a floating orb, called the sphere. Rudo is a bit of a bad kid. He's the child of a murderer, making him have no friends. The only thing that Rudo has are these old but nice gloves, adored with a specific symbol Living in the slums he has to steal from the upper class to survive. One day, a couple of masked people with wings, like angels, come and murder his stepdad. By a series of unfortunate events, preexisting prejudice and a betrayal from his only friend, Rudo gets sentanced to death for the murder. On the sphere, this is typically done by throwing people off and lettinng be the weapon. Or so the spherites thought. Rudo survives and learns that there is land, called the ground, below the sphere, full of all the garbage the spherites been throwing off the sphere to "no longer be their problem". Now Rudo must survive in this hostile environment, filled with toxic air and monsters made of literal garbage (where the series name comes from btw. In Japanese "ガチ" or gachi means actual or legit and "アクタ" means trash or garbage) with nothing but himself, his gloves, a heart full of reage and a mind full of vengeful thoughts.






My favourite character

We have returned to the trend of the best acharacter in the anime being a traumatised side character... but damn. I dare anyone with a soul and heart to watch Amo's backstory and not immediately lose it. She starts off as an antagonist. Not villain, she's not evil but simply in the way of the heroes. Yet even then you could tell that something is off, that no sane person would act like this. And you would be right. Amo has gone through a lot. I won't spoil what it is, because I want you to watch it first hand, but if the whole anime is heavy, this is lead. That said, even from just Amo's character design, we can be clued into what this poor girl expierienced.

The first thing you should bring attention to is her legs.She wears ribbons on her thighs, that look painfully stapled on almost. The intention was probably to envoke corset piercings, popular in Asian subculture, however those are usually temporary and the only time we see Amo without them was before she got them, and this looks rougher and more painful than those piercings irl. This reflects her twisted and unstable state of mind. Simultaneously, they bring to mind sheer stockings, which along with the pure amount of skin showing on her design, it's clear the author, Urana is going for a level of fanservice here... which is unfortunately fitting with what she went through... although her age being officially listed as "unknown" makes me uncomfortable. Which is exactly what it should do. Without going too far into it, Amo's character has 2 sides: a traumatised adult and a child that is in over her head. So while it is weird and creepy, like a lot of anime is, this one time it has a character reason behind it. Following this, her clothes (or what there is in their place) it turns out, is what's left of her childhood clothes that she grew attached to, unable to throw them away. The tattered dress, then is a symbol of her trauma andf distorted sense of self worth, while also showing her more mature side with the blatant sexualisation
